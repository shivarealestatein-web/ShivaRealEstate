## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-trust digital presence for real estate consultation and property development in South India, with an emphasis on Bengaluru's thriving land and residential market. Combining architectural precision with generational dependability, the aesthetic bridges vernacular accessibility (bilingual Kannada and English clarity) with modern executive luxury.

The core emotional objective is immediate legitimacy and security. Real estate decisions represent significant family and commercial investments; hence, frivolous trends are discarded in favor of refined architectural structure, deep oceanic sapphire tones, polished champagne gold markers, and crisp, luminous white layouts. 

The stylistic classification is **Corporate Modern with Classical Heritage Accents**. It features razor-crisp card surfaces, structural line rules inspired by architectural blueprints, understated elevation through ambient sapphire shadows, and disciplined dual-script vertical rhythm.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high-contrast trust, legal legitimacy, and premier asset ownership:

- **Primary (`#0F2854` - Deep Architectural Sapphire):** Anchors headers, primary action buttons, key banners, and high-impact structural panels. Communicates institutional permanence, stability, and legal certainty.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27` - Royal Warm Gold):** Reserved for prestige status cues, verified seal highlights, premium pricing tags, plot demarcation highlights, and conversion triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#1E58A4` - Cobalt Trust):** Used for interactive link states, active tabs, icon counters, and secondary functional accents derived from classic consulting signboards.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1B222C` - Basalt Slate):** Applied to primary body copy, subheaders, and micro-borders, replacing harsh pure black with rich stone tonality.
- **Surface & Backgrounds:** Crisp porcelain white (`#FFFFFF`) forms the foundational surface, layered over subtle architectural parchment grey (`#F6F8FB`) for background containers and alternating layout bands.
- **Semantic Accents:** Verified Emerald (`#0E8345`) for legal clear-title verification pills; Alert Carmine (`#C92A2A`) strictly for urgent plot reservation deadlines.

## Typography

The dual-script typographic system ensures visual harmony between Kannada and English scripts. Kannada glyphs require comfortable ascender and descender clearances; line-height tokens are generously proportioned (minimum 1.5x) to eliminate clipping of vowel signs (matras and vattus).

- **Headlines & Badges:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` paired with `Noto Sans Kannada`. Geometric, modern, yet humanized with broad terminals that lend confidence to high-value transactions.
- **Body & Data Specifications:** `Inter` paired with `Noto Sans Kannada`. Unsurpassed micro-legibility for dimensional metrics (sq. ft, guntha, survey numbers, approval IDs, and route distances).
- **Dual-Script Presentation Rule:** Primary headers display Kannada and English in stacked or inline configurations. When stacked, the Kannada script is displayed above or alongside the English text at an identical optical weight, with matching tonal color.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 8pt structural rhythm within a 12-column responsive fluid grid that caps at `75rem` (1200px) on large screens for comfortable visual scanning.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile:** `< 640px` (4-column grid, 16px screen gutters). Compact listing layouts, single-column inventory cards, sticky bottom action bar for instant calling/WhatsApp inquiries.
  - **Tablet:** `640px - 1024px` (8-column grid, 24px gutters). Two-column property cards and segmented category switchers.
  - **Desktop:** `> 1024px` (12-column grid, 24px gutters, max width 1200px centered). Three-column asset cards, split filter and inventory directory, horizontal verified hero section.

Layout density is designed to be purposeful: breathing room between property listings reinforces premium stature, while property meta-specs (facing, approvals, dimensions) remain tightly bundled in atomic data grids.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is built through architectural tonal layering rather than heavy synthetic drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** Clean `#F6F8FB` canvases and nested property detail modules.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting atop slate-tinted hairpins (`border: 1px solid rgba(15, 40, 84, 0.08)`) with a subtle ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 40, 84, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Listing):** Dynamic elevation shifting `translateY(-4px)` accompanied by `0 14px 30px -4px rgba(15, 40, 84, 0.12)`, drawing immediate focus to plot availability.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Navbars, Booking Drawers & Modals):** Pure white or deep sapphire backdrops paired with multi-layer ambient dispersion: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(15, 40, 84, 0.22)`.
- **Prestige Overlays:** Architectural elements (site layouts, blueprints, master plans) employ an ultra-fine 1px gold hairline (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.35)`) to signal elite commercial and gated-community tiers.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (`1`)** shape language with 4px to 8px radii across structural components. Real estate assets, land parcels, boundary surveys, and legal agreements are fundamentally rectilinear. Overly rounded or playful geometries dilute corporate trust and investment seriousness.

- **Cards, Modals & Outer Containers:** Soft 8px (`0.5rem`) radius providing disciplined containment without looking dated.
- **Action Buttons & Inputs:** 6px (`0.375rem`) radius to produce sharp, confident tap targets.
- **Badges, Pills & Verified Seals:** Semi-pill or strict 4px (`0.25rem`) corner rounding to maintain institutional badge aesthetics.

## Components

### Action Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Schedule Site Visit / Call Consultant"):** Deep sapphire background (`#0F2854`), crisp white text, bold gold hover ring (`#C59B27`). Minimum height 48px on mobile for thumb accessibility.
- **Secondary CTA ("Download Master Plan / View Survey"):** Transparent ground with 1.5px architectural sapphire border (`#0F2854`), primary colored text. Hover transitions to sapphire tint background (`rgba(15, 40, 84, 0.05)`).
- **Emergency / Instant Contact:** Direct WhatsApp and phone triggers rendered in high-contrast gold (`#C59B27`) with dark sapphire typography.

### Badges & Verification Seals
- **Clear Title / BDA / BMRDA / E-Khata Badges:** Emerald green tint background (`#EBF8F0`) with solid emerald text (`#0E8345`), preceded by an SVG shield checkmark icon.
- **Category Badges:** "Individual Plot", "Residential Layout", or "Commercial" rendered in soft sapphire pill containers (`rgba(15, 40, 84, 0.08)`) with deep sapphire text.

### Property Listing Cards
- Built using a structured vertical stack:
  1. **Visual Ratio Top (16:10):** High-resolution site image or layout boundary map, overlaid with category pill (left) and verified trust seal (right).
  2. **Card Body:** Dual-language property title (Kannada headline stacked above English subhead), location tag with pin icon (e.g., *Yelahanka, Bagalur Main Road*).
  3. **Specification Bar:** 3-column micro-grid displaying Dimension (e.g., 30x40), Facing (East/North), and Approval authority in neutral basalt tone.
  4. **Footer:** Prominent price bracket in bold sapphire next to a gold-accented "Enquire Now" action.

### Input Fields & Lead Capture Forms
- Generous form fields (46px height) with light grey surfaces (`#FDFEFE`), crisp 1px borders in basalt-tinted grey, and high-contrast sapphire focus rings (`2px solid #0F2854`).
- Floating labels supporting both Kannada and English placeholders (e.g., "Full Name / ಪೂರ್ಣ ಹೆಸರು").

### Filter Chips & Navigation Tabs
- Flat, rectangular-soft switchers enabling immediate toggling between **All Plots (ಎಲ್ಲಾ ನಿವೇಶನಗಳು)**, **Residential (ವಸತಿ)**, and **Commercial (ವಾಣಿಜ್ಯ)**. Active chips feature sapphire fill with gold active indicator pips underneath.
